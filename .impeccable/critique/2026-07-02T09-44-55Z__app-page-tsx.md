## Findings (🔴 critical / 🟡 moderate / 🟢 optional)

### 🔴 Critical

1. **Mojibake (double-encoded UTF-8) in 7 files, user-visible.** navbar.tsx, home-content-sections.tsx, projects-section.tsx, tech-stack-section.tsx, work-content.tsx, newsletter-section.tsx, reading-list.tsx. Renders live as "âŒ˜K" (search kbd), "View Resume â†'", "Reading list â†'", "Read notes â†'", "All work â†'", "Systems-level fundamentals â€"" and aria-label "Kawas â€" Home" (read aloud by screen readers). Verified in the running DOM.
2. **WCAG AA contrast failures across all secondary text.** Dark mode: white/45 ≈ 3.9:1, white/40 ≈ 3.3:1, white/35 ≈ 2.7:1, white/30 ≈ 2.3:1 on the near-black bg — nav links, section labels, card meta, descriptions all under 4.5:1. Light mode: text-gray-400 ≈ 2.5:1, text-gray-500 ≈ 4.4:1. About page: black/55 at 9–10px serif. PRODUCT.md promises "WCAG AA minimum."
3. **Reduced motion not honored.** Only home-content-sections checks useReducedMotion. LiquidGlassBg (5 infinite blobs), newsletter blobs (4), theme toggle (infinite orbit + ring pulse), navbar, tech tiles all animate unconditionally. PRODUCT.md promises reduced-motion for all framer-motion animations.
4. **Performance: the page never goes idle.** 21 blur(90–140px) blobs animated via JS (framer-motion) plus 29 backdrop-filter surfaces (blur 28–48px + saturate) on the homepage alone. Screenshot tooling timed out waiting for idle. Mobile GPU/battery cost is severe. Animate blobs with CSS transforms only, cut blob count, and reduce backdrop-filter surfaces.
5. **Dead links:** Medium/Hashnode/Dev.to icons in work-content.tsx point to "#".
6. **Theme toggle bug:** uses `theme` instead of `resolvedTheme` with defaultTheme="system" — wrong icon and a no-op first click for system-dark users.

### 🟡 Moderate

7. **Register contradiction:** PRODUCT.md declares "dark-native: the dark theme is the brand" but layout ships defaultTheme="system" and a full light theme where the glass barely reads (white/32 glass on 0.98-lightness bg).
8. **Identity fracture:** liquid-glass homepage vs annotated-paper about page (hard-coded text-black, 8–10px type, ignores dark tokens) vs experience-section legacy bg-card style.
9. **Eyebrow scaffolding on every section** ("Currently reading / Notable Projects / Technology Stack / Professional Experience") — the banned AI-grammar cadence. Detector: 21 AI-palette + 7 gradient-text hits across 12 files.
10. **Gradient text on the key h2 tagline** (also about h1, work headings) — banned pattern; pink-400→blue-400 on white is also low contrast in light mode. Carry the gradient in rules/underline/CTA, not headline glyphs.
11. **Glass as default, not accent.** Every card, tile, chip, and badge is a glass surface. Liquid Glass works when chrome floats over content; here the only thing "under" the glass is the blobs. Keep the navbar island; flatten most cards.
12. **Experience section content-empty:** two cards with company + date, no role; fixed grid-cols-2 cramped at 320px; arbitrary red/green icon colors.
13. **Resume is an 11px tertiary link** inside the Tech Stack header — recruiters are a stated primary audience.
14. **About page micro-typography:** 8px uppercase labels, 9–10px serif body, absolute annotation at right-[-18px] overflow risk on mobile; four competing emphasis styles in one paragraph.
15. **Newsletter section:** generic filler copy ("Thoughtfully curated insights…", "growing community of curious minds") violating the "no filler copy" principle; entrance uses `animate` on mount so it plays before the below-fold section is seen; infinite arrow bounce.
16. **"Build. Learn. Iterate." appears three times** (hero, footer, about).
17. **Anchor semantics confused:** nav 'experience' → '/#experience' never gets an active state and jumps cross-page; hero section id="about" while /about is a page.
18. **Small touch/read targets in navbar:** 14px social icons, 9.5–12.5px text.
