# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Developers, recruiters, and curious readers landing on this portfolio. Context: desktop browser, work hours, evaluating technical background and personality.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio and writing home for Kaustubha (Kawas). Showcases backend engineering and ML projects, reading notes, and professional experience. Success = visitors reach out or subscribe.

## Brand Personality
Sharp, curious, understated. Technical depth without showing off.

## Anti-references
Generic "Full Stack Dev" portfolios with hero metrics. SaaS-cream warm-neutral backgrounds. Eyebrow-on-every-section scaffolding.

## Design Principles
1. Dual-native theming: dark and light are both first-class; every glass surface must stay legible on each canvas (system default respected)
2. Heat gradient as the sole expressive accent (fuchsia → rose → amber); no purple/blue/cyan brand accents
3. Motion that reveals, never decorates
4. Density that respects intelligence: no filler copy

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA minimum. Reduced-motion alternatives for all framer-motion animations.
